# KraCar logo exploration v4

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Three directions were compared: architectural K, integrated wordmark, and interlocking K/C monogram. The K/C direction was selected for its distinctive silhouette and potential as a standalone icon. This is a design assessment, not a measured user preference study.

## Exploration prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create THREE genuinely distinct original logo candidates for KraCar, arranged as three clean horizontal rows on a white design review sheet, separated by generous whitespace. Exact wordmark in every row: "KraCar". All black / very dark charcoal #202624. No labels, numbers, explanatory captions, mockups or decoration. Each row contains a small symbol on the left plus a carefully kerned wordmark, balanced to work as a website header logo. Top direction: architectural K monogram built from three confident geometric strokes with a tiny deliberate diagonal cut, paired with a beautifully balanced medium weight geometric sans wordmark, confident and restrained. Middle direction: distinctive typographic KraCar wordmark with an integrated custom K that forms a subtle forward motion motif, no separate large icon, slightly rounded terminals, warm sophisticated mobility identity, medium-bold weight. Bottom direction: compact original interlocking K/C monogram using clever clean negative space, paired with elegant bespoke medium sans lettering, refined and memorable. Each must feel designed by a top independent identity studio, with optical balance, crisp vector-like edges and a different underlying idea. Premium but friendly Moroccan mobility marketplace. Avoid stock font K symbols, oversized icons, generic front-view car drawings, roads, lane markings, wheels, map pins, swooshes, gradients, shadows, 3D, textures, slogans and tiny flourishes. Prioritize ownable simple forms, strong silhouette at 24px, air between letters, typography that is neither thin nor excessively heavy. Render three thoughtful proposals, one per row, flat solid ink on pure white.

## Selected logo prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Finalize ONLY THE BOTTOM LOGO from the supplied three-row exploration sheet. Preserve its distinctive interlocking K/C monogram: an upright K stem on the left, diagonal inner negative-space cut, and a clear generous C-shaped curved right side. Preserve the bottom wordmark's clean geometric humanist style and exact text "KraCar". Create one polished horizontal lockup: symbol on left, wordmark on right, aligned optical center; symbol approximately 1.15 times the capital height, deliberate comfortable symbol-to-wordmark spacing, slightly more breathing room between letters than the exploration, medium-bold rather than ultra-heavy. Balance the symbol stroke thickness with the wordmark. Refine optical kerning, smooth curves and the diagonal negative-space notch. The result should be distinctive, sophisticated, simple, welcoming and legible at small website-header sizes. Single absolutely uniform solid charcoal #202624. Flat crisp vector-style artwork on a genuinely transparent background. Generous transparent margins, horizontal composition roughly 4:1. No top or middle design from the reference, no additional options, no labels, no numbers, no slogan, no illustrations, no cars, no road, no gradients, no lighting, no shading, no texture, no shadows, no glow, no 3D, no mockup. Output only one finished logo based on the BOTTOM K/C concept.
